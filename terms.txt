## 1. The Service

Spiral Breath+ is a structured breathwork practice app that provides:

- Configurable breath sessions with phase timing, audio cues, and visual guidance
- Real-time breath detection through your device's microphone
- Coherence scoring across six tiers
- Generation of unique geometric sigils from your session inputs
- A Breath Map and archive of your practice history
- Practice journeys (multi-day arcs)
- A library of curated presets, with additional content available under the Spiral Breath+ subscription
- Optional AI Reflection using your own Anthropic API key
- QR-code exchange of breath exercises

The App is available in two tiers — a free tier with a curated set of features and Spiral Breath+ ("Plus"), a paid subscription that unlocks the full feature set.

**Spiral Breath+ is a wellness app, not a medical device.** It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Information and features within the App are for general wellness purposes and educational interest only and are not medical advice.

Breath practices can produce strong physical and psychological effects. **Consult your healthcare provider before beginning any breathing practice**, especially if you:

- Are pregnant
- Have a history of cardiovascular disease, high or low blood pressure, or stroke
- Have asthma, COPD, or any chronic respiratory condition
