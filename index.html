For those who go deeper

Your session
becomes a
conversation.

No other breathwork app does this. After every session, your complete practice data — breath ratios, coherence scores, timing patterns, affirmations, Codex Field readings — is available as a JSON export.

Paste it into Gaia Architect+ or Orion Architect. They read your field, analyse your current state, and return a completely new personalised exercise built for exactly where you are right now. Import it back. Your next session is a direct response to your last one.

AI can help design the practice.
You still take the breath.

For users who want to go deeper. Neither is required — Spiral Breath works entirely on its own. Gaia Architect+ requires a paid subscription on gaia.com; Orion Architect offers a 7-day free trial on orionarchitect.io plus paid subscriptions for deeper use.

Copying a session or exercise out to an Architect works on both plans. Pasting a reply straight back in requires Spiral Breath+; on the free plan you can enter it manually.
